RatTADStask

A 3D model of a rodent navigation task. You play the rat: run in the wheel to open the doors, choose an arm, and lick the water if you chose right.

This game hasn't been tested or optimized for phones and tablets yet. It needs a keyboard and mouse. Here's a video of the gameplay:
